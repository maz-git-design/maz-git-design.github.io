Projects

Research and technical work in microelectronics, digital systems, and embedded computing.

This portfolio spans In-Memory Computing, VLSI and IC Design, FPGA and Digital Systems, Embedded Systems, and AI Hardware. The projects below document work undertaken; broader research interests are described on the Research page.

SRAM-Based In-Memory Computing for Tsetlin Automata

Investigation of training-aware SRAM-based in-memory computing architectures for hardware-efficient Tsetlin Automata state updates.

Energy-Efficient Solar Charging System

Design of a portable solar charging system combining Perturb and Observe maximum power point tracking with constant-current and constant-voltage charging control.

Real-Time Operating System Development

Development of a real-time operating system in assembly language, including task scheduling, interrupt handling, synchronization, and system timing mechanisms.

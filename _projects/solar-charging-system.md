## Overview

Design of a portable solar charging system combining Perturb and Observe (P&O) maximum power point tracking (MPPT) with constant-current and constant-voltage (CC-CV) charging control.

## Control Approach

The work brings together solar power-point tracking and battery charging control within a portable charging system. Implementation details and performance measurements are documented in the associated paper.
